# Contact Form Layout Design

## Goal

Remove the three guidance items from the contact form section and eliminate the empty space their removal would leave behind.

## Design

The contact section will use a single centered column. The existing "Reach out to us" heading and introductory paragraph will sit above the form, centered within the same pink section. The white form panel will remain, with a maximum width that keeps its fields comfortable to scan on desktop while continuing to fill the available width on mobile.

The guidance-item markup and its unused icon imports will be removed. Existing form fields, submission behavior, acknowledgement messaging, colors, and mobile stacking will remain unchanged.

## Verification

Browser coverage will confirm that the removed guidance text is absent and that the section uses the centered layout. Existing contact form submission tests and the production build must continue to pass.
